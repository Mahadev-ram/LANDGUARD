**Important:** The wiring drawing is provisional. The photo shows the physical prototype and slope model, but visible wiring does not establish every ESP32 GPIO assignment. Confirm every wire, sensor pin label, power rail, and common ground before uploading/powering the integrated sketch. Do not connect signals above 3.3 V to ESP32 GPIO/ADC pins.
